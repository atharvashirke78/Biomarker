model: balanced_updown_score_log2CPM
score(sample) = mean(log2(CPM + 1) over the UP genes) - mean(log2(CPM + 1) over the DOWN genes); CPM = featureCounts count / sample's featureCounts assigned total * 1e6
call tumour if score > cutoff (strictly greater); normal otherwise
n_up = 8, n_down = 1 (genes and signs in final_model_genes.tsv)
cutoff = 1.352325902   (median of the 5 inner-fold Youden cutoffs: 1.35233, 1.2408, 1.35233, 1.35233, 1.35233)
seed = 42 (LASSO CV folds) and 43 (inner cutoff folds); K = 5; folds stratified by group x study
lasso: glmnet binomial alpha = 1, lambda.1se = 0.0095792166 (cv.glmnet deviance), candidate features = the 1,390 final candidates (rules 1, 2, 3a, 3b at 18 of 19; 1390 genes in the matrix)
training samples: the 19 development samples (results/04_counts/counts_featurecounts.tsv); the 15 validation samples were not used
